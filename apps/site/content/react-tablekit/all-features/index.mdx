Each capability below is one page with a runnable demo, its variations, the ways it can be
customised, its known limitations and links to the generated reference.

**What is in scope:** rendering, querying and interacting with tabular data, in the browser or
against a server. **What is deliberately out:** fetching (bring your own), charting, pivot tables
and spreadsheet-style editing. Inline editing is specified and planned — see the
[roadmap](/react-tablekit/discover-more/roadmap/).

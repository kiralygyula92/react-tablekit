Capability pages answer "how do I use this?". These answer "how should I use it, and what happens when it grows?".

- [Server data](/react-tablekit/guides/server-data/): writing a data source, and everything the table does for you around it.
- [Best practices](/react-tablekit/guides/best-practices/): the handful of decisions that determine whether a table stays fast and predictable.
- [Performance](/react-tablekit/guides/performance/): what costs what, and the measured budgets.
- [Testing](/react-tablekit/guides/testing/): how to test a table you did not write.
- [Accessibility](/react-tablekit/guides/accessibility/): what is handled for you, and what stays yours.
- [Security](/react-tablekit/guides/security/): cell rendering, CSV export and untrusted data.

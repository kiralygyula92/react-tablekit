## Decisions required before Phase 2

These cannot be resolved by the agent:

1. **G-05 / G-06** — the standard assumes a commercial, tiered plugin with a marketing surface. This is a single-tier MIT library with no marketing site, no pricing and no testimonials. Confirm which of PPDS sections 1–11 and archetypes D, G and H are **omitted as not applicable** (each becomes an `EXCEPTIONS.md` entry), rather than filled with invented data.
2. **G-11 / G-23** — merging guides and examples into one capability page per PPDS archetype B changes every documentation URL. Confirm the new taxonomy before Phase 2 writes slugs that "never change again" (PPDS R3).
3. **G-16 / G-35** — moving content from TSX to Markdown-with-frontmatter is what makes the reference generator, `llms.txt`, the `.md` twins and the nav model possible. It is the largest single piece of Phase 3/5 work. Confirm before scaffolding.
4. **G-04** — with no published URLs, confirm that `migration/url-map.csv` covers internal links only.
